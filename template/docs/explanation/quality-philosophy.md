# Quality philosophy

Why each gate exists, and what it would cost to remove it. The page explains the
reasoning behind specification-first changes, the coverage floor, the split between the
read-only hook gate and the mutating fix configuration, the preference for local
evidence over network calls, and the rule that a failing gate is fixed at its root, with
a suppression kept as a stated last resort.
